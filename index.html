Flagship product

Nietzsche AI

An autonomous coding partner, not an autocomplete. Point it at a repo and it reads the whole thing, proposes the change, writes it, tests it, and tells you why.

§ I
Context

Full-repo, not open-tab

Nietzsche AI ingests your whole codebase before it touches a single line — dependencies, conventions, and history included, not just whatever file happens to be open.

§ II
Execution

Autonomous, not advisory

It doesn't just suggest a diff. It opens the branch, makes the change, runs your test suite, and reports back — you review the result, not every keystroke.

§ III
Self-critique

It reviews its own work

Every change is checked by the same model that wrote it, hunting for the parts it got wrong before you ever have to.

§ IV
Interface

Lives where you work

Terminal-native and CI-friendly — not another browser tab you have to remember to open and copy code out of.

How it thinks

The three metamorphoses.

Borrowed from Zarathustra: how a spirit becomes a camel, a lion, and finally a child — and how Nietzsche AI runs on every task, then loops again.

Stage 01

The Camel

It kneels and takes on the weight: your repo, your conventions, your open issues. Full-context ingestion, carried fully before a single change is proposed.

"What is heavy? ... the spirit that would bear much."

Stage 02

The Lion

It says "I will" to the fix instead of waiting on permission. Writes the patch, runs the tests, contests its own bad first draft.

"To create new values — that even the lion cannot do."

Stage 03

The Child

The PR opens: innocence, forgetting, a new beginning. A self-propelling wheel — it ships, and the loop starts again on the next task.

"A self-propelling wheel, a first movement, a sacred Yes."
